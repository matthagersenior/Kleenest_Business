# AGC of Missouri — Emerging Supplier / Service Provider Reference (2026)

_Source: `MemberApp_EmergingSupplierServiceProvider2026.pdf`, received as an attachment in the Kleenest/AGC outreach thread._

This is an **external partner/channel reference**, not a Kleenest-authored document.

## Why it matters to Kleenest

AGC Missouri's Emerging Supplier/Service Provider program provides a possible structured entry into the construction ecosystem for Kleenest as a small supplier/service provider. The form explicitly asks what services are most important to the applicant, including:

- networking opportunities,
- business-development opportunities,
- advocacy/governmental affairs,
- safety services/training,
- workforce-development training,
- information/webinars/forums,
- committees/work groups.

This aligns with the Kleenest construction/Fleet validation objective: meet contractors, learn field-worker problems, and recruit pilots without asking AGC to select or endorse individual companies.

## Eligibility / program economics captured in the 2026 document

The document describes an Emerging Supplier/Service Provider tiered/discounted program for firms whose reported annual work volume remains at or below the stated program threshold.

The dues overview shows:

| Program year | % of base dues | 2026 listed amount |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 30% | $311 |
| 2 | 45% | $466 |
| 3 | 60% | $621 |
| 4 | 75% | $776 |
| 5 | 90% | $932 |

The same document also contains a separate first-page line stating “First year dues: $320.00 prorated quarterly.” Because those two figures conflict inside the source document, **confirm the actual current amount with AGC before paying or quoting it**.

The document lists the 2026 base as $1,065 and notes that the emerging pricing can last up to five years if the firm's annual work volume remains within program limits.

Treat AGC membership as a **channel/customer-discovery investment**, not traction by itself. Measure:
- contractor conversations,
