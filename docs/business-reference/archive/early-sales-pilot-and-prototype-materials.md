# Early Sales, Pilot & Prototype Business Materials

These materials predate the current v9/v10 strategy but preserve how Kleenest's commercial story evolved.

## One-page pitch deck

_Source: Kleenest_One_Page_Pitch_Deck.pptx, August 2026._

Early positioning:
- “The cleaner way to find a public restroom.”
- Problem: no trustworthy cleanliness signal; generic/scattered ratings; businesses lack direct incentive/tools around restroom experience.
- Product: cleanliness score, recent cleaning, ratings/reviews, amenities/accessibility, and a business dashboard.
- Early business model: two-sided marketplace with free consumer discovery and paid business visibility/tools.
- Early proposed business subscriptions: **$19–$49/month**.
- Sponsored placement/local advertising.
- Premium verification, analytics, and promotions.
- Immediate next step: pilot with 10–25 local businesses.

This pricing is historical and is superseded by current pricing/reference material.

## St. Louis Pilot Deck v2

_Source: Kleenest_STL_Pilot_Deck_fixed_v2.pdf / .pptx._

Pilot goal:
- 10–25 St. Louis locations,
- validate QR check-ins,
- collect evidence,
- prove repeat consumer value,
- use a 30-day first evidence window.

### Pilot loop
Find → Check in → Verify → Return.

### Partner value
- Claim/improve profile
- Verify amenities
- Use QR for engagement
- Test promotions/contests/rewards
- Learn what people search for and verify

### Data advantage
Source → Observation → Evidence → Verification → Confidence → Freshness → Contradiction.

### Pilot ask
- 10–25 businesses/venues/community locations
- warm local introductions
- investor-readiness feedback after proof exists

The deck explicitly intended to package evidence for partners, Arch Grants, and angels.
