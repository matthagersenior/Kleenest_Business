	Key Observations and Implications for AdSense:
* Commercial Focus: Keywords with "commercial" in them tend to have higher CPCs, suggesting that businesses are willing to pay more to reach this audience. If your app targets businesses, focusing on content and user flow that attracts this segment could be beneficial for AdSense revenue.
* Service vs. Product: Keywords related to "services" (e.g., "restroom cleaning services") generally have higher CPCs than those related to "supplies."
* Location Specificity: Including location-based keywords (e.g., "restroom cleaning services near me" - not shown in the table but important to consider) can also increase CPC as advertisers target specific geographic areas.
* App-Specific Keywords: Keywords directly related to finding clean restrooms or apps might have lower CPC but are crucial for attracting your target users. You might see ads from competing apps or related services.
Next Steps:
1. Refine based on your app's focus: Does your app primarily target commercial clients, individual users seeking clean public restrooms, or both? This will help you prioritize certain keyword themes.
2. Consider the user journey: At what points in the app's usage will users see ads? The context of the ad display can influence click-through rates. For example, a user actively searching for a cleaning service might be more likely to click on a relevant ad.
3. Experiment with ad formats: Different ad formats (banner, interstitial, native) have varying CPMs and CTRs. Experimenting will be key to optimizing revenue.
