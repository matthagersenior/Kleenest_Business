# Kleenest Business Reference Library

This directory is the repository-local reference/data-room for Kleenest business, market, financial, partnership, pilot, outreach, pitch, and research material.

## Purpose

Keep business context close to the Business product without mixing it into runtime code.

- `Kleenest_Architecture/main` remains the canonical source for product behavior, service contracts, schemas, authorization, and shared platform design.
- This directory is **reference material**, not executable product truth.
- Current strategy is separated from historical material so an older pitch, price assumption, or forecast is not accidentally treated as canonical.
- Source artifacts discovered in the connected Kleenest Library/Drive are indexed in [CATALOG.md](./CATALOG.md).
- Google Drive originals that have durable Drive URLs are listed in [SOURCE_LINKS.md](./SOURCE_LINKS.md).

## Historical material

Earlier v3-v8 business plans, decks, models, outreach plans, pilot decks, and meeting materials are preserved in the catalog and summarized in [archive/HISTORICAL_MATERIALS.md](./archive/HISTORICAL_MATERIALS.md).

## Reference rules

1. Prefer the highest current version for strategy, pricing, and forecasts.
2. Do not treat seeded/imported location records as customer traction.
3. Enterprise and paid-restroom-access revenue are upside unless a later canonical plan explicitly promotes them into the core forecast.
4. Re-check external studies, competitor pricing, market statistics, and partner facts before using them in externally distributed material.
5. Do not copy private contact data or confidential correspondence into public-facing material without review.
