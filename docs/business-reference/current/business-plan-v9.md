# Kleenest Detailed Business Plan v9 — normalized reference

_Source artifact: Kleenest_Detailed_Business_Plan_v9.pdf / .docx, September 2026._

## Executive positioning

Kleenest starts with a universal consumer need: finding and trusting restroom access in the real world. The free consumer product is the adoption engine. Consumer use creates current, verified restroom intelligence; businesses and employers then pay to understand, influence, improve, and operationalize that network.

The v9 operating plan is intentionally conservative about unproven revenue. Paid restroom access and Enterprise contracts are treated as upside rather than core revenue.

## Product architecture

### Consumer network
- Free restroom discovery and route-aware utility
- Verified observations, check-ins, freshness, and confidence signals
- Optional Premium and Family upgrades

### Business platform
- Claimed locations and restroom profiles
- Restroom intelligence, QR workflows, and customer-experience signals
- Analytics and business response tools

### Growth / Fleet
- Premium employee access bundled into B2B plans
- Routing, dispatch, assignment, arrival confirmation, and verification
- Affordable FSM-like on-ramp for mobile workforces

### Enterprise
- Multi-location and complex-facility requirements
- Integrations, SLA requirements, API/SSO, advanced permissions, custom reporting
- Hardware-agnostic IoT / smart-restroom integration path

## Pricing and packaging

| Product | Planning price | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Business Standard | $20/location/month | Core |
| Business Growth | $50/location/month | Up to 5 locations before Enterprise |
| Fleet | $75/account/month | Includes Premium employee access |
| Consumer Premium | $5 one-time | Core |
| Consumer Family | $20 one-time | Core |
| Enterprise | Custom publicly | Internal floor starts at $499/month; upside only in v9 forecast |

Growth can support 50 included Premium seats per location. A five-location Growth account is $250/month and may provide up to 250 seats, roughly $1/employee/month at full utilization before valuing intelligence, routing, dispatch, task coordination, or service verification.

Fleet at $75/month with 50 included Premium seats is roughly $1.50/employee/month at full utilization before valuing the operations layer.

## Validation milestones

- Prove consumer repeat use.
- Prove verified observations become current and trustworthy.
- Prove businesses pay after local network value exists.
- Validate Growth/Fleet employee-benefit economics.
- Validate the issue → dispatch → service → verification loop.
- Scope Enterprise value before quoting beyond the floor.
- Test paid access separately; do not rely on it to make the plan viable.

## Go-to-market

Prioritize:
- consumer-density accelerators,
- chambers and business networks,
- mobile-workforce employers,
- field-service operators,
- hospitality and venues,
- facilities and janitorial partners.

The ask varies by partner: app distribution, introductions, customer discovery, or operational pilot.

## Risk controls

- Seeded/imported locations are coverage infrastructure, **not traction**.
- Do not present paid access as proven.
- Do not claim Kleenest replaces full FSM/TMS/ELD products.
- Do not push Enterprise pricing before requirements are scoped.
- Protect the free consumer experience from over-monetization.
