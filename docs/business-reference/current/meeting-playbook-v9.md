# Kleenest Meeting Playbook v9 — normalized reference

_Source artifacts: Kleenest_Meeting_Playbook_v9.pdf / .docx._

## Cortex / SQ1

- Lead with the consumer-first operating plan and detailed financial model.
- Ask mentors to challenge the Base case, not admire the deck.
- Do not lead with Four Seasons, paid access, or Enterprise upside.
- Goal: leave with one 14-day experiment and a 10-week scoreboard.

## Four Seasons

- Use the Four Seasons-specific offer deck, not the general financial model or internal roadmap.
- Lead with guest-experience signals, optional branded QR, no-signage baseline, service recovery, and operational visibility.
- Do not present SMB pricing, consumer growth targets, Fleet seat economics, or company-wide projections unless asked.
- Discuss Enterprise only as pilot-to-Enterprise path; internal floor starts at $499/month and final scope depends on zones, integrations, support, and service requirements.

## Chambers / employers

- Lead with low-cost Premium access plus affordable routing, dispatch, and verification.
- Relevant Growth example: 5 locations = $250/month and up to 250 included Premium seats.
- Avoid paid restroom access unless the prospect owns controlled restroom capacity and raises monetization.
