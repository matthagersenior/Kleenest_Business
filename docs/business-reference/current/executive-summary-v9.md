## Positioning

Kleenest is a consumer-first restroom-intelligence and affordable field-operations platform. The free consumer app is the adoption engine: people use it to find reliable restroom access, generate verified observations, and create current local data density. Business subscriptions, Growth/Fleet accounts, sponsorship, and later Enterprise deployments monetize the network.

## Core financial philosophy

- Core revenue = Standard, Growth, Fleet, consumer upgrades, conservative ads, and sponsorship.
- Premium seats bundled into Growth/Fleet are not double-counted as consumer revenue.
- Enterprise is custom-priced and excluded from the v9 core forecast until scoped or contracted.
- Paid restroom access is optional upside and is not required for the plan to work.
- Aggressive Growth is explicitly capital-intensive.

## Enterprise quoting discipline

Publicly: custom pricing.

Internally: begin with a **$499/month floor**, using approximately a $250 platform fee + $40/location/month subject to that minimum; separately scope onboarding, integration, IoT, API, SSO, custom reporting, advanced support, and custom workflows.

## SQ1 / mentor ask

The correct near-term discussion is validation, not fundraising. Pressure-test:
- consumer repeat use,
- verified restroom observations,
- B2B conversion,
- Growth/Fleet employee-benefit economics,
