_Source artifacts: Kleenest_Specific_Outreach_Plan_v9.pdf / .docx._

## Campaign posture

Do not sell paid restroom access as the primary business model. Lead with:
- free consumer adoption,
- verified restroom density,
- business / operations value,
- bundled Premium employee access,
- affordable FSM-like workflows.

Paid access should appear only where a venue naturally raises controlled-access economics.

## Segment playbook

| Segment | Example targets | Primary ask | Lead value | Avoid leading with |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cortex / SQ1 | SQ1 mentors | Stress-test 10-week validation sprint | Attack assumptions in Base model | Fundraising / paid access |
| Chambers / business networks | Hispanic Chamber, AACC, North County, Chesterfield, South County | Introduce 5-10 mobile-workforce or multi-location members | Employee benefit + low-cost operations | Generic app-store pitch |
| Mobile-workforce employers | Couriers, trucking, delivery, home health, contractors, utilities | 30-60 day workforce pilot | Restroom/location utility + routing/dispatch/verification | Full TMS/ELD/FSM replacement claim |
| Janitorial / facilities | Cleaning companies, facility managers, property operators | Issue-to-resolution pilot | Dispatchable restroom-condition signals + proof | Consumer reviews alone |
| Venues / events | Art fairs, races, festivals, conventions | Consumer-density activation | Immediate local utility and verified observations | Enterprise software pitch |
| Hospitality | Four Seasons St. Louis and hotel groups | Guest-experience/service-recovery pilot | Discreet service signals, optional QR, operational visibility | Generic roadmap / SMB pricing |

## Positioning rules

- Tie each ask to the recipient: introduction, pilot, activation, or operations proof.
- Use the ~$1/employee/month Growth example only where employee-benefit economics matter.
- Use the $499+ Enterprise floor only for complex sites, multi-location portfolios, integrations, or SLA discussions.
- Never count seeded locations as traction.
- Do not present paid access as proven; label it optional future upside.
