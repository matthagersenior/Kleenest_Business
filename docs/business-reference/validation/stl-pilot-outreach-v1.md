# Kleenest St. Louis Pilot Outreach

## One-sentence positioning

Kleenest is a St. Louis-built verified local-discovery platform that helps people find places with the amenities they actually need, then improves that data through QR check-ins, evidence, freshness and business participation.

## Pilot objective

Activate 10–25 St. Louis businesses, venues or community-facing locations, collect real QR check-ins and amenity evidence, measure repeat usage, and package the results for partner, Arch Grants and angel-investor conversations.

## Primary CTA

Send prospects to `/pilot` and ask them to choose one of five paths:

- Business pilot location
- Community validator / early user
- Startup mentor / advisor
- Local introduction source
- Investor / capital connection

## Metrics to capture

- Pilot locations activated
- Amenity facts verified
- QR scans / check-ins
- Searches completed
- Searches with successful amenity match
- Repeat visitors / return usage
- Business partner feedback
- Contradictions resolved
- Evidence freshness by location

## Founder meeting ask

Do not open with “fund my app.” Open with:

> I am building Kleenest in St. Louis and want to prove it here first. I am looking for pilot partners, sharp feedback, and the right introductions so we can generate evidence before asking anyone for capital.

## 30-day action sequence

### Week 1

Open the pilot page, send targeted outreach, recruit first business conversations and identify 10–25 candidate locations.

### Week 2

Onboard first pilot locations, validate core amenity fields, test QR check-ins and collect first observations.

### Week 3

Measure searches, scans, verifications, returns, contradictions and partner feedback.

### Week 4

Package proof for Arch Grants readiness, local angel feedback and civic/business partnership conversations.
