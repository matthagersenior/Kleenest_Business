# Kleenest Investor / Grant / Mentor View

This is the shortest defensible diligence path for someone evaluating Kleenest as a company rather than as a single pilot.

## 1. Company overview

Read:
- [Executive Summary v9](../business-reference/current/executive-summary-v9.md)
- [Business Plan v9](../business-reference/current/business-plan-v9.md)

The company story should remain consumer-first: consumer usage and geographic density create the network; Business, Fleet, Enterprise, advertising, APIs/SDKs, and other monetization layers build on that network.

## 2. Market and evidence

Read:
- [Market Evidence & Sizing](../business-reference/current/market-evidence-and-sizing.md)
- [Independent Market / Competitive Analysis](../business-reference/research/independent-market-competitive-analysis-2026-08-28.md)

Use the independent analysis as historical outside-in context, not as current verified competitor truth.

## 3. Financial model

Read:
- [Financial Model v9](../business-reference/current/financial-model-v9.md)

Use v9 for pricing, assumptions, revenue architecture, expense structure, and forecasts unless a newer approved model is added.

## 4. Product and defensibility

Key defensibility themes in the business corpus:
- verified practical location intelligence,
- source/evidence/provenance,
- confidence and freshness,
- contradiction handling,
- physical-world verification,
- business-managed information,
- QR/check-in activity,
- route-aware utility,
- consumer-density network effects,
- API/SDK/widget/map/route/deep-link/webhook/AI platform potential.

Do not reduce the company to “a restroom app” when discussing the long-term platform thesis; do not obscure that restroom discovery is the initial consumer wedge.

## 5. Validation and traction discipline

Read:
- [SQ1 Founder Prep](../business-reference/validation/sq1-founder-prep-v1.md)
- [Opportunity Readiness & Funding](../business-reference/validation/opportunity-readiness-and-funding.md)
- [Skandalaris Workshop Pack](../business-reference/validation/skandalaris-workshop-pack.md)

The validation system deliberately distinguishes product/data infrastructure from live adoption.

## 6. St. Louis proof-market strategy

Read:
- [STL Pilot Outreach v1](../business-reference/validation/stl-pilot-outreach-v1.md)
- [37-Target Materials Manifest](../business-reference/validation/target-materials-manifest.md)

Investor framing preserved from the readiness work:

> St. Louis is the proving ground, not the market.

The objective is to prove repeatable adoption, participation, data quality, willingness to pay, and expansion mechanics.

## 7. Fundraising readiness

Use:
- current pitch material,
- current financial model,
- bottom-up market logic,
- pilot KPIs,
- customer-discovery notes,
- signed confirmations/LOIs,
- cap table,
- formation/IP documents,
- privacy/security summary,
- use-of-funds plan.

See [DILIGENCE_CHECKLIST.md](./DILIGENCE_CHECKLIST.md).

## 8. Claims that should remain conservative

Until current evidence supports them, do not claim:
- national adoption,
- meaningful revenue,
- business/customer counts based on ingested places,
- a Four Seasons partnership,
- association endorsement,
- certified accessibility,
- validated willingness to pay from outreach interest alone.

## 9. Ask discipline

A financing ask should specify:
- amount,
- instrument/structure under consideration,
- milestone the money buys,
- estimated runway,
- evidence already achieved,
- next proof point unlocked.

Do not use fundraising to substitute for customer proof.
